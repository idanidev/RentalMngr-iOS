Renting out rooms? This app was built for exactly that.
Every room with its own tenant, rent and expenses, instead of a generic "property" app where a shared flat never quite fits.
Paperwork included: lease as PDF, a listing for the empty room, and a yearly report for your tax return.

PER ROOM, NOT PER PROPERTY
• Every room with its tenant, its rent and its status: occupied or free
• Common areas kept separate: kitchen, living room, terrace...
• Photos and inventory for each room
• Real occupancy at a glance

PAPERWORK SORTED
• Lease as PDF with your own clauses and variables: rent, deposit, dates
• Room listing as PDF, with photos and your contact details
• Yearly report per property: collected, pending, expenses and net
• Documents stored per property and per tenant

CLEAR RENT AND EXPENSES
• Each room's rent, marked as collected or pending
• Electricity, water, gas... or a flat service fee with what it covers
• Expenses by category and a monthly summary across all your properties

NOTHING SLIPS THROUGH
• Alerts for this month's rent, pending deposits and empty rooms
• Contract-expiry alerts, with the notice period you choose
• Weekly summary

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
• iPhone, iPad and Mac, synced instantly
• Share a property with your partner, business partner or manager, with edit or read-only access
• Face ID or Touch ID lock

Free: 1 property and 2 rooms. Premium: no limits.
